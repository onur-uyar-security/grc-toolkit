# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI Management System (AIMS) Overview
 
**ISO/IEC 42001:2023** is the first international standard for an **AI Management System
(AIMS)**. It helps an organisation **govern the development and use of AI responsibly** —
addressing risk, transparency, accountability and the AI lifecycle. It shares the
**Harmonised Structure** (clauses 4–10) with ISO/IEC 27001, so the two integrate well.
 
## Why it matters
- Provides a certifiable framework for **responsible / trustworthy AI**.
- Complements emerging AI regulation (e.g. the EU AI Act) with a management-system approach.
- A rare, high-value credential area — few practitioners hold an **ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Auditor** certification.
## Management-system clauses (4–10)
Same structure as ISO 27001: **Context · Leadership · Planning · Support · Operation ·
Performance evaluation · Improvement** — applied to AI.
 
## Key concepts specific to AIMS
| Area | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **AI policy** | Organisation's stated objectives and principles for responsible AI |
| **AI roles & accountability** | Clear ownership for AI systems across the lifecycle |
| **AI risk assessment** | Risks from AI to the organisation **and** to individuals/society |
| **AI system impact assessment** | Assessing impacts on people, groups and society (fairness, safety, rights) |
| **Data for AI** | Data quality, provenance, governance for training/operation |
| **AI lifecycle** | Requirements, design, development, verification, deployment, operation, monitoring, retirement |
| **Transparency & information** | Information to users and affected parties about AI use and limits |
| **Third-party / supplier AI** | Governance of externally provided AI components and services |

## Quick AIMS readiness checklist
- [ ] AI policy approved and communicated
- [ ] AI systems inventoried, each with an owner
- [ ] AI risk assessment method defined and applied
- [ ] AI system impact assessments performed for higher-risk systems
- [ ] Data governance (quality, provenance, access) in place for AI data
- [ ] Human oversight and monitoring defined for AI in operation
- [ ] Supplier/third-party AI components governed
- [ ] Internal audit and management review cover the AIMS
