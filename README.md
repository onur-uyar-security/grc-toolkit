# GRC Toolkit — ISO/IEC 27001 · ISO/IEC 42001 · NIS2
 
A practical **Governance, Risk & Compliance** toolkit: generic templates, checklists and
notes for building and auditing an **Information Security Management System (ISMS)** and an
**AI Management System (AIMS)**, and for preparing for **NIS2**.
 
Built from my work as an **ISO/IEC 27001 & ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Auditor**. All content is
**generic and reusable** — it contains no real organisational data; replace the
placeholders (`<...>`) with your own context.
 
## What's inside
 
| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| [`iso27001/`](iso27001) | ISMS risk-assessment methodology, Statement of Applicability (Annex A 2022) template, internal-audit checklist (clauses 4–10) |
| [`iso42001/`](iso42001) | ISO/IEC 42001 AI Management System overview and key control areas |
| [`nis2/`](nis2) | NIS2 readiness checklist (Article 21 measures) and reporting timeline |
| [`templates/`](templates) | Risk register template, ISO 27001 ↔ NIS2 control mapping |
 
## How to use
1. Copy the relevant template into your own (private) workspace.
2. Replace every `<placeholder>` with your organisation's context.
3. Keep evidence (records) for each control — auditors assess *what you do*, not *what you wrote*.
## Scope & disclaimer
These are **educational templates** reflecting the structure of the referenced standards and
the NIS2 Directive. They are a starting point, **not legal advice** and **not a substitute**
for the official standards (ISO/IEC 27001:2022, ISO/IEC 42001:2023) or Directive (EU) 2022/2555.
