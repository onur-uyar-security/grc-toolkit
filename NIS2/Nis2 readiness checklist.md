# NIS2 — Readiness Checklist
 
**Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2)** raises the baseline for cybersecurity risk management
and incident reporting across the EU. This is a generic readiness checklist — confirm your
**scope, obligations and national transposition** with the competent authority / legal
counsel.
 
## 1. Am I in scope?
- [ ] Determine whether the entity is **essential** or **important** (based on sector + size).
- [ ] Check **Annex I / Annex II** sectors (e.g. energy, transport, banking, health, digital
      infrastructure, ICT service management, public administration, postal, waste,
      manufacturing of critical products, digital providers, research).
- [ ] Apply the **size-cap rule** (generally medium-sized and above) and any national exceptions.
- [ ] Register with the competent authority as required.
## 2. Risk-management measures (Article 21 — minimum set)
- [ ] (a) Policies on **risk analysis** and information system security
- [ ] (b) **Incident handling**
- [ ] (c) **Business continuity** — backup, disaster recovery, crisis management
- [ ] (d) **Supply chain security** (including supplier relationships)
- [ ] (e) Security in **acquisition, development and maintenance**, incl. **vulnerability handling and disclosure**
- [ ] (f) **Policies to assess the effectiveness** of cybersecurity risk-management measures
- [ ] (g) **Basic cyber hygiene** and **cybersecurity training**
- [ ] (h) **Cryptography** and, where appropriate, **encryption**
- [ ] (i) **Human-resources security**, **access control** and **asset management**
- [ ] (j) **Multi-factor / continuous authentication**, secured comms and emergency communications
## 3. Incident reporting (Article 23 — timeline)
- [ ] **Early warning** to the CSIRT/competent authority within **24 hours** of becoming aware
- [ ] **Incident notification** within **72 hours** (with an initial assessment)
- [ ] **Final report** within **1 month** of the notification
- [ ] Process to notify **recipients** of services where appropriate
## 4. Governance & accountability
- [ ] **Management bodies approve** the cybersecurity risk-management measures and oversee implementation
- [ ] Management receives **cybersecurity training**
- [ ] Accountability for non-compliance is understood (NIS2 strengthens management liability)
## 5. Mapping to ISO/IEC 27001
Most Article 21 measures map to **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A** controls — see
[`../templates/control-mapping-iso27001-nis2.md`](../templates/control-mapping-iso27001-nis2.md).
An existing ISMS is a strong head start for NIS2.
