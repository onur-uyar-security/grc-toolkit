# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — Internal Audit Checklist (Clauses 4–10)
 
A generic checklist for an **ISMS internal audit (clause 9.2)**. For each item, record
**Conformity** (C / NC / OFI) and **evidence** seen.
 
> C = Conformant · NC = Non-conformity · OFI = Opportunity for improvement
 
## Clause 4 — Context of the organisation
- [ ] 4.1 Internal/external issues relevant to the ISMS are identified
- [ ] 4.2 Interested parties and their requirements are determined
- [ ] 4.3 ISMS **scope** is documented and justified
- [ ] 4.4 The ISMS is established, implemented, maintained and improved
## Clause 5 — Leadership
- [ ] 5.1 Top management demonstrates leadership and commitment
- [ ] 5.2 An **information security policy** exists, is approved and communicated
- [ ] 5.3 Roles, responsibilities and authorities are assigned and known
## Clause 6 — Planning
- [ ] 6.1.1 Risks and opportunities for the ISMS are addressed
- [ ] 6.1.2 A documented **risk assessment** process exists and is applied
- [ ] 6.1.3 A **risk treatment** process exists; the **SoA** is complete and justified
- [ ] 6.2 Information security **objectives** are set, measurable and planned
- [ ] 6.3 Changes to the ISMS are planned
## Clause 7 — Support
- [ ] 7.1 Resources for the ISMS are provided
- [ ] 7.2 Competence is determined; records of training/experience kept
- [ ] 7.3 Staff are aware of the policy and their contribution
- [ ] 7.4 Internal/external communication is planned
- [ ] 7.5 Documented information is controlled (versioning, access, retention)
## Clause 8 — Operation
- [ ] 8.1 Operational planning and control; outsourced processes controlled
- [ ] 8.2 Risk assessments performed at planned intervals and after change
- [ ] 8.3 Risk treatment plan implemented; results documented
## Clause 9 — Performance evaluation
- [ ] 9.1 Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation are defined and performed
- [ ] 9.2 **Internal audits** are planned and conducted; results reported
- [ ] 9.3 **Management review** is held; inputs/outputs recorded
## Clause 10 — Improvement
- [ ] 10.1 Continual improvement of the ISMS
- [ ] 10.2 Non-conformities are corrected; **corrective actions** taken and verified
## Annex A (sample spot-checks)
- [ ] A.5.1 Information security policies are approved and communicated
- [ ] A.5.15 Access control policy applied (least privilege, need-to-know)
- [ ] A.5.7 Threat intelligence is collected and used
- [ ] A.8.8 Technical vulnerabilities are identified and remediated (SLA)
- [ ] A.8.16 Monitoring/logging in place; alerts reviewed
- [ ] A.6.3 Security awareness training delivered; records kept
