# Control Mapping — NIS2 (Article 21) ↔ ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A
 
An **indicative** mapping showing how NIS2 Article 21 risk-management measures align with
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls. An existing ISMS covers much of NIS2 — this helps you
reuse evidence. Mapping is **not one-to-one**; adapt to your context.
 
| NIS2 Art. 21 measure | Indicative ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls |
|---|---|
| (a) Risk analysis & information system security policies | A.5.1 Policies; clause 6.1.2 Risk assessment |
| (b) Incident handling | A.5.24–A.5.28 Information security incident management |
| (c) Business continuity, backup, disaster recovery, crisis management | A.5.29 ISMS during disruption; A.5.30 ICT readiness for BC; A.8.13 Information backup |
| (d) Supply chain security | A.5.19–A.5.22 Supplier relationships & ICT supply chain |
| (e) Secure acquisition, development & maintenance; vulnerability handling | A.8.25–A.8.29 Secure development; A.8.8 Technical vulnerability management |
| (f) Policies to assess effectiveness of measures | Clause 9.1 Monitoring & measurement; 9.2 Internal audit; A.5.35 Independent review |
| (g) Cyber hygiene & training | A.6.3 Awareness & training; A.8.7 Protection against malware |
| (h) Cryptography & encryption | A.8.24 Use of cryptography |
| (i) HR security, access control, asset management | A.6.1–A.6.6 People; A.5.15–A.5.18 Access control; A.5.9–A.5.11 Asset management |
| (j) MFA / continuous authentication, secure communications | A.8.5 Secure authentication; A.8.20–A.8.21 Network & network services security |
 
## How to use
1. For each NIS2 measure, confirm the mapped ISO controls are **implemented** (see your SoA).
2. Reuse the **evidence** you already keep for ISO 27001 to demonstrate NIS2 measures.
3. Close any gaps where NIS2 expects something your current scope does not cover
   (e.g. reporting timelines in Article 23).
> Indicative mapping only. Verify against the official texts of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 and
> ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and your national NIS2 transposition.
